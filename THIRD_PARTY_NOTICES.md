# Data rights and attribution

MIT covers this project's original code and documentation. It does not replace
the licenses of source observations, geometries, books, or linked publications.
The catalog and published source inspector retain per-source rights statements.
Catalog discovery is not a redistribution grant.

- **Natural Earth:** bundled 1:110m country geometry was taken from the War Maps
  snapshot. Natural Earth data are public domain. Attribution: Natural Earth,
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Borders reflect the
  source's cartographic choices and are not an endorsement of territorial claims.
- **World Bank WDI:** population observations were copied from the frozen War
  Maps WDI snapshot with original indicator IDs and years. World Bank datasets
  are generally CC BY 4.0 with dataset-specific exceptions. Review the indicator
  metadata and https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/legal/terms-of-use-for-datasets .
- **RTLDI Atlas:** published country model outputs are attributed to Sid J.A.
  Hubbard and the 2026 RTLDI Atlas. The source README licenses code,
  documentation, and generated outputs MIT. Its theoretical books retain their
  own CC BY-NC-ND terms and are not copied or relicensed here. Underlying V-Dem
  and World Bank material retains its terms. This kit does not recreate RTLDI's
  regression or relabel model estimates as observed capital losses.
- **UCDP:** the event example retains individual provisional candidate records,
  source versions, and estimates from War Maps, attributed to UCDP and the
  upstream reporting offices retained in each event. No candidate fatality
  total is published. Consult https://ucdp.uu.se/downloads/ for version-specific
  documentation, citation, and terms before a different redistribution/use.
- **Global Life-Support Atlas:** original project code/documentation MIT; no
  accepted observation data existed at the examined commit. The reference
  includes explicit missingness, not fabricated life-support findings.

Other source-library records include sources with registration, restricted
redistribution, or manual review requirements. They are references only unless
an actual observation is explicitly incorporated. In particular, do not mirror
commercial news, imagery, restricted health datasets, or proprietary ship tracks
merely because a source URL is public.
